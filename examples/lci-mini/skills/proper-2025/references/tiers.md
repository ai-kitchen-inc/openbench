# PROPER 2025 Rating Tiers

PROPER uses five color-coded tiers. Companies are rated annually by KLHK.

## Hitam (Black) — Non-Compliant
- Operates without permits, violates major environmental regulations
- Penalty: administrative sanctions, potential operation halt
- LCA: not applicable — company must first achieve compliance

## Merah (Red) — Partial Compliance
- Documented effort toward compliance, still violating minimum standards
- LCA: typically not required at this tier
- Focus: get to Biru first (pollution control baseline)

## Biru (Blue) — Compliant (Baseline)
- Meets all minimum environmental regulations (emissions, waste, water)
- Baseline rating — most industrial companies operate here
- LCA: optional but valuable for moving upward

## Hijau (Green) — Beyond Compliance
- Implements environmental management beyond legal minimums
- **LCA becomes important**: demonstrates quantified impact reduction
- Required: ISO 14001 certification, community development program,
  quantified resource efficiency improvements
- Typical LCA use: cradle-to-gate assessment showing year-over-year
  emissions reduction per product unit

## Emas (Gold) — Excellence in Environmental Management
- Top tier; only 1–3% of rated companies achieve this
- **LCA is mandatory** for most industries
- Required: full LCA per ISO 14040/44, environmental innovation,
  community impact measurement, global benchmarking
- LCA submission must include:
  - Clear system boundary (cradle-to-gate minimum)
  - Functional unit (usually per ton product or per MJ)
  - All 17 standard LCI categories covered
  - Pareto analysis of environmental hotspots
  - Third-party verification or critical review
