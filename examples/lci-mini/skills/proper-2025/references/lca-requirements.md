# PROPER 2025 LCA Submission Requirements

For companies targeting Hijau or Emas ratings, the LCA submission must
address the following categories. Each line states the KLHK expectation.

## Mandatory Coverage (17 LCI Categories)

Inputs:
1. Bahan Baku (Raw Materials) — natural resource extraction
2. Air (Water) — consumption per production unit
3. Bahan Pendukung Cairan — liquid additives, catalysts, solvents
4. Bahan Pendukung Padatan — solid inputs other than main raw material
5. Transportasi — fuel consumption for transport of supporting material
6. Fuel Gas — natural gas, fuel gas for process heat
7. Bahan Bakar Cair — diesel, fuel oil, gasoline
8. Listrik — electricity (grid + on-site generation)
9. Infrastruktur — annualized allocation of plant infrastructure
10. Lahan — land occupation in m² or m²a

Outputs:
11. Produk — main and co-products (required for allocation)
12. Sampah — non-hazardous solid waste
13. Limbah B3 — hazardous waste (requires B3 manifest docs)
14. Limbah Cair — wastewater
15. Emisi Udara — CO2, CH4, NOx, N2O, SOx, PM, nmVOC, TOC

Ratings (computed):
16. Hotspot Pareto — top 80% contributors per category
17. Functional Unit — per MJ (PROPER standard for energy products) or per
    ton product (standard for commodity manufacturing)

## Documentation Requirements

- Signed LCA report in Bahasa Indonesia (English translations allowed)
- Data source traceability (primary measurement, secondary database, LCI
  database citation)
- Uncertainty declaration (±% for measured, database quality for secondary)
- Allocation method justification (mass, economic, or energy)
- System boundary diagram

## Common Rejection Reasons

- Missing N2O data for processes that use nitric acid or ammonia
- Unit mixups (amounts in ton not converted to kg for aggregation)
- No baseline year comparison (Hijau requires YoY improvement)
- Fabricated emission factors without source citation
