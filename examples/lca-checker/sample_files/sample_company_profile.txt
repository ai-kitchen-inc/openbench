PRODUCTS
================================================================
1. Corrugated cardboard boxes (3-layer, 5-layer)
   - B-flute, C-flute, E-flute configurations
   - Standard and custom dimensions
   - Used for: food, electronics, consumer goods shipping

2. Recycled paper bags
   - Brown kraft paper, 80 gsm
   - Retail and industrial applications

RAW MATERIALS
================================================================
- Virgin pulpwood: sourced from FSC-certified plantations (Sumatera)
- Recycled fiber: OCC (Old Corrugated Containers) from local collection
  Recycled content: 55% average across products
- Starch adhesive: corn-based, locally sourced
- Printing inks: water-based, low-VOC
