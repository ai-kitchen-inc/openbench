================================================================
         LIFE CYCLE ASSESSMENT REPORT
         PT Green Packaging Indonesia
================================================================

Study ID: LCA-2024-001
Date: March 2024
Prepared by: Environmental Management Division

1. GOAL AND SCOPE DEFINITION
================================================================

1.1 Goal
The goal of this LCA study is to quantify the environmental impacts
of corrugated cardboard box production at PT Green Packaging Indonesia
for the purpose of Environmental Product Declaration (EPD) publication.

1.2 Functional Unit
1 kg of corrugated cardboard box, 3-layer, B-flute, dimensions
600x400x300mm, suitable for shipping consumer goods.

1.3 System Boundary
Cradle-to-gate: from raw material extraction (pulpwood harvesting and
recycled fiber collection) through transport to mill, pulping process,
corrugating medium production, and box converting/packaging.

Excludes: distribution to customer, use phase, and end-of-life treatment.

1.4 Allocation Procedures
Mass-based allocation applied to co-products from the pulping process.
Recycled fiber input credited using the recycled content method.

1.5 Impact Categories Selected
- Global Warming Potential (GWP) - IPCC AR6
- Acidification Potential (AP) - CML-IA
- Eutrophication Potential (EP) - CML-IA
- Photochemical Ozone Creation Potential (POCP) - CML-IA
- Ozone Depletion Potential (ODP) - WMO 2022
- Abiotic Depletion Potential (ADP) - CML-IA

1.6 Data Quality Requirements
- Primary data: production records from 2023 (12-month average)
- Background data: Ecoinvent 3.9.1
- Geographic scope: Indonesia (foreground), Global (background)
- Temporal scope: data less than 2 years old
- Completeness target: >95% of mass and energy flows

1.7 Cut-off Criteria
Flows contributing less than 1% of total mass, 1% of total energy,
or 1% of environmental relevance may be excluded.

2. LIFE CYCLE INVENTORY (LCI)
================================================================

2.1 Data Collection
Primary data collected from PT Green Packaging production facility
in Tangerang, Banten for calendar year 2023.

Key inputs per kg corrugated cardboard:
- Virgin pulpwood: 0.45 kg
- Recycled fiber: 0.62 kg
- Process water: 8.5 L
- Electricity (PLN grid): 0.42 kWh
- Natural gas: 0.15 m3
- Starch adhesive: 0.035 kg

2.2 LCA Software
SimaPro 9.5 with Ecoinvent 3.9.1 database

2.3 Indonesian Grid Emission Factor
PLN national grid: 0.794 kg CO2eq/kWh (ESDM, 2023)

3. LIFE CYCLE IMPACT ASSESSMENT (LCIA)
================================================================

LCIA Method: CML-IA baseline v4.8

Results per functional unit (1 kg corrugated cardboard box):

  Impact Category    Value           Unit
  ─────────────────────────────────────────
  GWP                0.89            kg CO2 eq
  AP                 0.0045          kg SO2 eq
  EP                 0.0012          kg PO4 eq
  POCP               0.00035         kg C2H4 eq
  ODP                2.1E-08         kg CFC-11 eq
  ADP                0.0028          kg Sb eq

Dominant contributors to GWP:
- Electricity consumption: 42%
- Natural gas combustion: 28%
- Transport: 15%
- Raw material extraction: 12%
- Other: 3%

4. INTERPRETATION
================================================================

4.1 Significant Issues
- Electricity consumption is the largest contributor to GWP (42%)
- Recycled fiber content significantly reduces virgin material impact
- Transport distances for recycled fiber collection affect AP and EP

4.2 Completeness Check
Data completeness: 95% of identified mass and energy flows captured.
Minor gaps: packaging materials for internal transport (<0.5% of total).

4.3 Sensitivity Analysis
Sensitivity to electricity grid factor: +/- 10% change in grid factor
results in +/- 4.2% change in total GWP.
Sensitivity to recycled content: increasing recycled content from 55%
to 75% reduces GWP by approximately 12%.

4.4 Consistency Check
All foreground processes use primary data from the same facility and
time period. Background data consistently sourced from Ecoinvent 3.9.1.
LCIA method (CML-IA) applied uniformly across all categories.

4.5 Conclusions
The corrugated cardboard box from PT Green Packaging shows GWP of
0.89 kg CO2 eq/kg, which is near the industry median (0.85 kg CO2 eq/kg).
Primary improvement opportunity: transition to renewable electricity.
